邀请测试服务条款

更新于 2026-09-27

只能连接你有权使用的账户、仓库和项目，发布你有权分发的内容。构建在你的 CI 账户运行，目标部署使用你选择的供应商账户。供应商配额、费用与服务条款独立适用。工作台不会替你自动签署链上交易。

静态站可导入；服务端渲染、API、定时任务或专有服务需要适配。兼容性报告不构成任意 Vercel 项目可无损迁移的保证。请验收预览后再切换正式域名。

测试期设有单次文件、项目数量、归档空间与登录频率限制。禁止滥用计算、上传恶意内容、攻击其他用户或越权读取数据。维护者可以限制异常活动。

Invitation testing terms

Updated 27 September 2026

TapeNow connects the code, build, and hosting services you authorize. This invitation-testing version offers no production SLA. Keep independent backups of source code and important data.

Connect only accounts, repositories, and projects you are authorized to use, and publish only content you may distribute. Builds run in your CI account and deploy to your selected provider account. Provider quotas, charges, and terms apply separately. TapeNow does not automatically sign chain transactions for you.

Static sites can be imported. Server rendering, APIs, scheduled jobs, and proprietary services require adaptation. A compatibility report is not a guarantee that any Vercel project can migrate without loss. Validate previews before switching production domains.

Testing limits apply to file sizes, project counts, archive storage, and sign-in frequency. Do not abuse compute resources, upload malicious content, attack other users, or access data without authorization. Operators may restrict abnormal activity.
