A 公测：发布到您选择的入口

当前为限量公开测试：每天最多 50 个新账号、首阶段总共 300 个；受邀账号仍可继续使用。TapeNow 帮助您导入、构建、临时预览、发布上链、核验、更新、恢复和导出。正式浏览使用 TapeKit、已验证的 BSC HashPort 或您自己的 Cloudflare。

新项目暂不提供 TapeNow 长期托管；已有自用和测试托管项目保留。免费预览不等于免费上链：gas 和上游激活费在执行前另行确认，均不是 TapeNow 收费。

从免费体验开始

  1. 创建项目，导入文件或生成示例 v1。
  2. 创建私有预览，保存仅显示一次的密码，再打开预览。
  3. 导入 v2；旧版本保留。恢复会创建候选版本，不会立刻修改线上文件或业务数据库。
  4. 正式上链时确认文件、链、容器和预算，由您的钱包签署。交易完成后读回核验所有文件。
  5. 打开已激活的 TapeKit 网址，检查版本、图片、链接和主要交互，再记录结果。链上内容证明、入口有效期和用户网页观察分别记录。
  6. 使用自己的 Cloudflare 时先连接对应账号；云端网页与链上文件分别发布，供应商费用由您的账号承担。

遇到中断

相同操作编号和相同输入可重取已有结果，不能用同一编号换参数。若提示“可能已执行”，先查看版本和操作记录。已广播交易保留哈希，不要重复签署。

TapeKit 使用浏览器 Service Worker 读链。HTTP 启动页不是游戏正常运行的证明，浏览器检查如实标为用户观察。多笔链上更新不是原子切换，期间可能出现新旧文件混合；没有通过检查就保持待确认。

A beta: publish to your chosen entry point

Access is a limited public beta: up to 50 new accounts per day and 300 in this stage; invited accounts remain supported. Import, build, preview, publish onchain, verify, update, restore and export in TapeNow. Visitors use TapeKit, verified BSC HashPort or your own Cloudflare.

New projects do not receive permanent TapeNow hosting in A beta. Existing internal and test sites remain available. Free previews do not mean free onchain publication: gas and upstream activation fees require separate confirmation and are not TapeNow fees.

Start with a free preview

  1. Create a project; import build files or generate sample v1.
  2. Create a private preview, save the password shown once and open it.
  3. Import v2 while retaining v1. Restore creates a candidate without immediately changing the live website or business database.
  4. Confirm files, chain, container and budget. Your wallet signs transactions; then verify every onchain file.
  5. Open the activated native TapeKit URL and check its version, images, links and interactions. Record your observation separately from the content proof and activation status.
  6. For your own Cloudflare, connect the appropriate account. Cloud and onchain files are published separately; your supplier account bears its charges.

Resume interrupted work

The same operation identifier and input retrieve the existing result; different input cannot reuse the identifier. If an operation may already have run, inspect versions and operation records. Broadcast transaction hashes stay saved; do not sign again.

TapeKit reads onchain files through a browser Service Worker. An HTTP boot page does not prove the game works. Browser checks are labeled as user observations. Multiple onchain writes are not an atomic switch; visitors may see mixed files during an update. Failed checks remain pending.
